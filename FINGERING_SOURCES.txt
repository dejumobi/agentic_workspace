SaxWorx fingering reference notes

Written pitches use the scientific convention C4 = MIDI 60.
Standard range: Bb3 through F#6. Tenor starter altissimo examples: G6 through C7.

Fingering facts checked against The Woodwind Fingering Guide:

SaxWorx's interface, key labels and simplified 3D geometry are original. No source chart images are bundled. Highlighting describes controls the player presses, rather than modelling every pad and linkage. A tenor starter is one possible route, not a universal fingering. Save your own combinations by note and instrument after checking against your saxophone and teacher's chart.

High F# uses the dedicated high F# key plus high-F palm fingering; a front-F alternate is provided for instruments without that key. Model layout is schematic and differs from individual saxophone brands.
