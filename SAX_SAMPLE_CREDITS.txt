Changes: leading silence trimmed; converted stereo WAV to mono MP3 at 128 kbps; cropped to 4.5 seconds. Nearest recorded whole-tone note was pitch shifted for intervening semitones. MIDI note numbers in this app use C4 = 60; source filenames use C3 for that same pitch. Below the recorded tenor range (concert Ab2, MIDI 44), notes are transposed from the lowest sample for other instrument selections.
Playback preserves the recorded attack, applies volume and release envelopes, and loops a sustained section for longer notes. Demonstrations are assembled individual recorded notes; they are not a live legato performance.

Previous FluidR3_GM soundfont samples have been replaced.
