YOUR PRACTICE, WITH A PURPOSE

From “what do I play?” to a session you can follow.

Bring your instrument. Open your studio in a browser. MofePlay gives you exercises and accompaniment to help you spend less time deciding what to practise and more time playing.

DIRECTION

Know your next step

Follow instrument-specific lessons with written steps, audio demonstrations and reference videos. Return to earlier lessons when you need another run-through.

LISTEN & PLAY

Turn an idea into practice

Hear examples, work through exercises on your own instrument, and track your lesson progress as you practise notes, rhythm, tone and coordination.

GROOVE

Make repetition musical

Rehearse alongside drum and bass grooves at a tempo you choose. Work on keeping time and fitting your phrases into an accompaniment.

THE MOFEPLAY DIFFERENCE

Learn a phrase.
Then give it a groove.

Guided lessons and creative practice belong together. Groove Lab lets you explore drum and bass patterns, adjust the tempo and build your own grooves. PianoWorx and GuitarWorx also offer groove rehearsal, so you can take an exercise into play-along practice.

Use the same notes at a slower tempo, try a different rhythm, and hear how your phrase fits the beat. It gives you a reason to keep playing beyond the first repetition.

WHO WE ARE

Built by a player, for people learning to play.

MofePlay is an independent music practice app created by a tenor saxophone player. It brings SaxWorx, PianoWorx and GuitarWorx together to help learners build a regular practice routine and enjoy making music. We are improving the lessons and tools with feedback from the people who use them.
