Before you begin

  1. Support the instrument so your fretting hand can move freely.
  2. Fretting fingers: index 1, middle 2, ring 3, little finger 4. Thumb rests behind the neck.
  3. Press close behind the fret wire using the lightest pressure that gives a clean sound.
  4. On bass, alternate plucking index and middle fingers and mute strings you are not playing.
  5. On classical guitar, p = thumb, i = index, m = middle, a = ring on the plucking hand.

Start slowly. Use small position shifts instead of forcing a wide stretch. Stop when uncomfortable.
